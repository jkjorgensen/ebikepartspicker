Privacy

Last updated Oct 18, 2026.

What the site does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts, and no advertising trackers.
  • No cookies set by this site, and nothing saved in your browser’s storage.

What happens when you use it

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site.
  • Fonts: fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when a page loads.
  • Your build: the parts you pick are stored in the page address (the part after #) so you can share a link. They aren’t sent to us.
  • Email links: "Contact", "Notify me", "Submit your part" and report links open your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply or act on your message.
  • Used parts: contacting a seller opens an email to that seller. Your message goes to them, not through us.
  • Retailer links: when you click through to a store, that store’s own privacy policy applies. Affiliate links (see the disclosure) let the retailer’s affiliate network record that the visit came from this site.
