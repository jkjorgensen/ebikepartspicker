Submission guidelines

Use “Submit your part” on the homepage or email us.

What we need

  • Product name and brand
  • A link to the product on the manufacturer’s or an authorized retailer’s site
  • Which bikes it fits, with a source that names them
  • Optional: the price you saw and the date

What we don’t accept

  • Links to copycat or unauthorized stores
  • Fitment claims with no source (they’ll be listed as Unknown fit)
  • AI-generated or edited product photos
  • Paid placement: submissions can’t buy a better status or ranking

Every submission is reviewed before it goes on the site, and the site may decline or edit submissions. If you sell the product, please say so.
