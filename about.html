About

eBikePartsPicker is built by Josh in St. George, Utah. He rides a Ventus One Plus, and the build on the homepage is his actual bike.

The site helps you plan an e-moto build part by part: pick a bike, choose parts, and see the total price, weight and estimated power in one place. There's also a bike finder, side-by-side comparisons, build guides and a power-upgrade page.

How we keep it honest

  • Every part shows a fit status. A part is only marked Confirmed fit when a seller or manufacturer listing names the bike, or the site owner has installed it on his own bike. See the fitment methodology.
  • Prices come from the retailer's product page and show the date they were checked. Prices and stock change, so always confirm on the retailer's page before you buy.
  • Some links are affiliate links. See the affiliate disclosure.
