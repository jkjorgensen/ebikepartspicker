Fitment methodology

Every part shows one of five statuses for the bike you picked:

✓ Confirmed fitA seller or manufacturer listing names this bike, or the site owner installed it on his own bike (marked “owner-verified”). Tap “Why?” in the builder to see the source.
○ Likely fit — verifyThere is a source, but it doesn’t clearly name your exact model (for example, it names the model family). Check with the seller.
△ Requires modificationThe source says it fits with an adapter, spacer or other change, which we note (for example, the EXT Ferro fork needs a hub boost spacer).
✗ Does not fitThe source says it doesn’t fit this bike.
? Unknown fitNo source names this bike. It might fit, but we can’t confirm it. Ask the seller.

Rules we follow

  • Nothing is marked Confirmed without a source. “It probably shares parts with…” is Unknown, not Confirmed.
  • Build guides and power combos are only published when every part is a Confirmed fit and every price was checked on the retailer’s page.
  • Power combos also need evidence that the parts work together: matching voltage range, connectors or a bike-specific harness, and mounting.
  • On Oct 18, 2026 we checked every part’s fit list against the seller’s listing text. Pairs the listing didn’t name were moved to Unknown. The full results are published as a CSV.
  • Checks are dated. Listings change, so treat every status as a snapshot.

Disagree with a status? Send us the source and we’ll update it.
